## The checklist the studio scores a 'make it a hit' ask against
- Danceability first: straight kick, medium syncopation in hats and bass, a clear downbeat every bar.
- Valence: a lifted hook (arch contour, small intervals, one twist), major color or a bright minor chorus.
- Energy under control: master in band, no stacked bright layers, dynamics kept for the drop.
- Form: first hook by bar 20 at the latest, about 3:20 total, eight chords or fewer, the hook stated three times.
- Era: pick the decade's opening progression and tempo band when the ask names an era.
