# SKILL: every genre has a DNA (quick reference; the ten studio lanes carry full recipes in LANE RECIPE)

Use this when the user names a genre outside the ten lanes or asks how a lane differs. Numbers are typical centers, not laws.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Tech-house | 124-128 | 4 on the floor, tight | rolling offbeat saw | 0.52-0.56 | minor | the bass IS the hook, sparse tops |
| Deep house | 118-124 | 4 on the floor, soft | sub with a 7th color | 0.55-0.6 | minor 7ths, jazzy | warm chords, long pads |
| Melodic house and techno | 120-126 | 4 on the floor | sub root, arps above | near straight | minor, modal | arpeggios, long builds, emotional breaks |

Rules: a genre named outside the ten lanes still needs a lane id for the engine; pick the nearest lane ("techno" for dubstep or drum and bass drums, "trap-club" for trap, "afro-house" for afrobeats, "deep-house" for disco and lo-fi) and adjust tempo and feel with ops.
