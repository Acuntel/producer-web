# Sample sources the producer can legally pull and play

Research date: 2026-09-02. Every license claim below was read from the source's own page on that date unless marked (U), which means the page blocked automated fetching and the claim rests on a search-engine summary or a third-party mirror. "Ok for app" means the sound may be shipped inside this commercial web app (public/samples, fetched into IndexedDB) and the rendered songs may be distributed, with no per-user license step.

The rule this project already follows (memory, 08-30) still holds: synthesize in the engine where possible; pull only CC0 or public-domain audio; keep a per-file provenance manifest; screen every file, because uploader-declared CC0 is a claim, not a guarantee. Skip anything named after a trademarked drum machine whose provenance is a re-sample of commercial software, and skip the Amen break in every form (a copyrighted recording regardless of the uploader's tag).

## The pull recipe (what worked today)

1. Search with Openverse: `curl "https://api.openverse.org/v1/audio/?q=riser&license=cc0&page_size=12"`. Read `url`, `foreign_landing_url`, `creator`, `attribution`, `duration`.
2. Confirm the license on the origin page: `curl -A "Mozilla/5.0" https://freesound.org/s/{id}/` and grep for "Creative Commons 0". Every one of the 29 Freesound sources in the starter pack showed it on 2026-09-02.
3. Fetch the audio: the `url` field (Freesound HQ preview, 128 kbps mp3, no auth), or raw GitHub for Sonic Pi and VCSL, or archive.org per-file for Producer Space.
4. Convert with ffmpeg: `ffmpeg -i in -af "silenceremove=start_periods=1:start_threshold=-45dB,volume=<gain>dB" -t <len> -ac 1 -ar 44100 -sample_fmt s16 out.wav` for one-shots; `-codec:a libmp3lame -b:a 128k` for loops and risers over 4 s. Gain comes from a `volumedetect` pass so every file peaks at -1 dBFS, the same reference the synthesized kit uses.
5. Record provenance in `public/samples/starter/manifest.json`. The manifest is an object: `pack`, `base`, `about`, `processing` (shared by every file), `samples` (per file: id, file, name, role, bpm, key, seconds, source, url, license, author, attribution, via, note, bytes) and `kits` (id, name, lane, note, sounds: kick/clap/snare/hat/openhat/perc mapped to sample ids). Roles: kick, clap, snare, hat, openhat, perc, vocal, keys, fx, riser, loop. `tests/samples.test.mjs` validates every entry (file present, bytes match, license allowed, role valid, kit sounds match their role, pack under 6 MB), so run it after every pull. The engine (`lib/engine/samples.ts`) reads the manifest at runtime, `lib/brain/contract.ts` lists it for the brain as SAMPLES AVAILABLE, and `lib/samples-attribution.ts` writes the credits file on download whenever a used sound's license asks for one (CC BY); CC0 needs none.

## The starter pack (public/samples/starter)

40 files, 3.6 MB, all 44.1 kHz mono, every file under 310 KB. Roles: 4 kicks, 2 claps, 3 snares, 4 hats, 2 open hats, 6 percussion, 7 vocals (shouts, sung Ab minor 124 BPM phrases, a spoken line, a C# minor 128 BPM chop loop), 2 keys (TX81Z FM piano C4 and Yamaha upright C4, root = midi 60), 3 FX, 3 risers, 4 loops (breakbeat 126, drum and bass 160, Compuspank 148, The Black Dog FX bed 130). Sources: Freesound CC0 (29 files, 24 of them through the Sonic Pi CC0 set, 5 through Openverse), VCSL (8), Producer Space (7), The Black Dog CC0 donation (2). Loop tempos marked "inferred from length" in the manifest were computed from the file duration and bar counts, not read from the source.

## What is not in the pack and why

No Amen break (copyrighted Winstons recording whatever the uploader says). No 808 or 909 "kits" re-sampled from commercial software. No Splice, Loopmasters, 99Sounds, Cymatics or BBC material. No CC BY files yet: the engine has no place to show credits, so the pack is CC0 only; add a credits panel before pulling CC BY sounds.
