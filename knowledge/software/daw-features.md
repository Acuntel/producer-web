# DAW features the studio still lacks: ranked, with acceptance specs (2026-09-02)

Scope: what working club producers actually reach for in Ableton, FL Studio and Logic that SongDoc v3 cannot express yet. Source markers [Dn] resolve in sources.md. Everything below is written in OUR vocabulary (ops, SongDoc fields, schedule/graph/voices behavior, one Node test). The law from lib/critic.ts holds throughout: nothing here auto-applies a musical judgement; every feature is an op or an action the brain returns when the user asks, and the critic may only recommend it.

## What exists today (so the gaps are real)
Tracks with roles, notes on the straight grid, named pattern variants chosen per section, synth params, insert fx (filter, drive, crush, delay, eq, comp, width, pan, trance gate), ONE reverb return, master chain (eq, filter, comp, makeup, limiter, clipper), one master-wide sidechain depth on every duck:true track, automation ramps (from, to, curve) per section, audio clips (GrainPlayer warp, semitones, startSec, reverse, one-shots with midi 60 = original), loop region, humanize (global swing on the 2nd and 4th 16th of a fixed role list, velVar on hats/snare/perc, clapLateMs, pocketMs, seed), fills cut/roll/riser, grid step notation, hum to MIDI, hearSong per section with a cache keyed on section + humanize + master + active tracks.

## Ranking rule
A feature ranks high when (1) a producer would say it in one sentence and expect it in one exchange, (2) it is deterministic and seedable (same seed, same feel forever; the ears' section cache depends on it), (3) it can be proven in Node with the existing test runner, and (4) it serves the laws in docs/VISION.md: human not perfect, every 8 bars something mutates, Ableton's power radically simplified.

## 1. Groove templates per track (Groove Pool)
Origin. Ableton's Groove Pool has Base (the grid the groove is measured against), Quantize 0-100%, Timing, Random ("random timing fluctuation" applied differently per voice), Velocity -100 to +100 (negative inverts), and a Global Amount up to 130% for exaggerated grooves; grooves apply to MIDI and to warped audio, Extract Groove reads timing and level from any clip, Commit writes the groove into the notes [D2][D24]. MPC templates are timing maps that shift notes off-grid forward or backward (MPC60, MPC3000, SP-1200, 909 families) [D31]. Practice: kick straight, main clap straight or nearly, hats, shakers, percussion, ghosts and possibly the bass swing; a 10-30% groove amount, a 52-56% feel for minimal tech house, Swing 16 rather than 8th shuffles, low or zero random [D29]; quantize at 25-50% to tighten without stiffening [D30]; Attack ships a custom groove as a MIDI clip with its tech-house kit [D18]. The process library already quotes swing per lane (CORE: tech house 0.56-0.60, garage 0.60-0.66, hard house 0.54-0.58 on hats only).
Gap. humanize.swing is one number applied to slot 2 and 4 of every beat for a hard-coded role list; no per-track amount, no 8th swing, no velocity map, no timing random, no groove taken from the reference.
Spec.
- types.ts: `export type GrooveName = "straight" | "swing16" | "swing8" | "mpc16" | "mpc8" | "909" | "sp1200" | "garage-skip" | "custom"`; `export type Groove = { name: GrooveName; amount?: number; velocity?: number; random?: number; timing?: number[]; accents?: number[] }` where amount is 0-1.3 (default 1), velocity is -1..1 (default 0.5), random is 0-12 ms, timing and accents are 16 entries per bar for custom (beats offset per 16th slot, velocity multiplier per slot).
- Song gains `groove?: Groove`; Track gains `groove?: Groove | null` (null = straight). Defaults by role: kick, clap, sub straight; everything else follows the song groove. `humanize.swing` stays as the swing16 shortcut and migrates: swing s becomes `{ name: "swing16", amount: (s - 0.5) / 0.17 }`.
- lib/song/groove.ts (pure): `templateOffsets(name)` returns `{ timing: number[16], accents: number[16] }` (mpc16 shifts slots 1 and 3 of each beat by +0.0833 beats at amount 1, which is 66%; mpc8 shifts slot 2; 909 shifts slots 1 and 3 by +0.05 and lifts slot 2 accents; garage-skip shifts slot 3 more than slot 1); `grooveFor(track, song)`; `applyGroove(local, vel, groove, rng)` returns `{ beat, vel }`.
- schedule.ts replaces the swingShift and velVar lines with applyGroove per event: `slot = round((local % 4) / 0.25) % 16`, `beat += timing[slot] * amount`, `vel *= 1 + (accents[slot] - 1) * velocity`, `beat += (rng() * 2 - 1) * random / 1000 / beatSec` from a per-track stream `mulberry32(seed ^ hash(track.id))` so editing one track never reshuffles another.
- ops.ts: `{"op":"groove","track"?: id|"drums"|"all","name":"mpc16","amount"?: 0.6,"velocity"?: 0.5,"random"?: 4}`; `{"op":"groove","track":"kick","name":"straight"}`; `{"op":"groove","track":"all","name":"custom","from":"reference"}` which useStudio resolves by injecting the reference groove before applyOps.
- lib/ears/groove.ts (pure): `extractGroove(onsetEnv, fps, bpm, phaseSec)` folds the onset envelope into 16 slots per bar and returns a custom Groove (timing = mean onset offset per slot, accents = mean level per slot, both normalized); factsForBrain adds one line "reference groove: offbeat 16ths +18 ms, accents 1.0/0.6/0.85/0.5".
- contract.ts adds: "GROOVE: one op sets the feel per track; mpc16 at 0.6 on hats and percussion, straight on kick and clap, from:reference to borrow the uploaded track's timing."
- The ears' measured swing stays the verify metric (`expect: [{"metric":"swing","dir":"up"}]`).
Test (tests/song.test.mjs). After `groove hat mpc16 amount 1`, every hat event whose straight position is slot 1 or 3 lands exactly `+0.0833` beats late, slots 0 and 2 are unmoved, kick events are identical to the straight schedule; amount 0 equals straight; two scheduleEvents calls with the same seed are deep-equal; `random: 5` never moves a note more than 5 ms at the song bpm. In tests/ears.test.mjs a synthetic 16th click train with the 2nd and 4th clicks delayed 20 ms extracts timing[1] and timing[3] within 3 ms of truth.

## 2. Pattern playlists per section (follow actions)
Origin. Ableton Follow Actions chain clips: Stop, Play Again, Previous, Next, First, Last, Any, Other, Jump, with Chance A/B and a Linked mode that fires at the end of the clip or after the Multiplier count of loops [D4]. Producers arrange 32-bar drops as four 8-bar cells A/B/C/D and mutate something every 8 bars (process 10); KETTAMA's cuts live on fills at the end of every 4 and 8 bars (process 10).
Gap. `section.variants[trackId]` is one pattern name for the whole section. A/B/C/D drop cells today mean four sections, which breaks the loop region, section naming, the ears' labels and the "Drop 2 = Drop plus one element" law.
Spec.
- types.ts: `export type VariantPlan = string | { sequence: string[]; barsEach?: number } | { every: number; pattern: string; bars?: number; base?: string } | { any: string[]; barsEach?: number }`; `Section.variants?: Record<string, VariantPlan>`.
- pattern.ts: `patternFor(track, sec, bar = 0)`: a string behaves as today; sequence plays `sequence[floor(bar / barsEach) % length]` (barsEach default 8); every plays `pattern` in the last `bars` bars (default 1) of each `every`-bar cycle and `base ?? "A"` elsewhere; any picks per cycle with `mulberry32(seed ^ hash(sectionId + trackId))`.
- schedule.ts: when a track's plan is not a string, tile per bar: for bar b the notes are `patternFor(tr, sec, b)` and only the events whose local position falls inside `[b*4, b*4+4)` are emitted, with the loop phase `(b * 4) % loopBeats` so 8-beat patterns still play whole.
- ops.ts: `variant` accepts a VariantPlan as `pattern`; sugar `{"op":"fill","section":id,"track":id,"every":8,"pattern":"fill","bars":1}` writes `{ every, pattern, bars }`; `grid` with `pattern: "fill"` writes the fill pattern.
- harmony.ts chordsForSection and render.ts sectionCacheKey already serialize the section, so chords per bar and the ears follow automatically.
- contract.ts adds: "VARIANT PLANS: {"sequence":["A","B","A","C"],"barsEach":8} for A/B/C/D drops, {"every":8,"pattern":"fill","bars":1} for a fill in the last bar of every 8, {"any":["A","B"],"barsEach":4} for seeded variety. Use a plan instead of splitting a section."
Test. A 16-bar drop with hat pattern A (16 notes per bar) and B (8 per bar) and `variants.hat = { sequence: ["A","B"], barsEach: 4 }` yields 64 hat events in bars 1-4, 32 in bars 5-8, 64 in bars 9-12, 32 in bars 13-16; `{ every: 4, pattern: "fill", bars: 1 }` places fill notes only in bars 4, 8, 12, 16; `any` with seed 42 gives deep-equal schedules on two calls and a different one with seed 43.

## 3. Note chance and velocity deviation, deterministic
Origin. Live 11 gives every note a Chance 0-100% and a Velocity Deviation range ("velocity 60 with a range of +20 plays 60 to 80, chosen each time the note plays") plus a Randomize button [D5]. Ableton's own guidance: velocity range on hi-hats, chance for "droplet-style sparseness" on arps and bell clusters, chance per chord for occasional flavor, lowered chance on chopped vocal slices for house ear candy [D17]. The Random MIDI effect (Chance, Choices 1-24, Interval, Add/Sub/Bi) and the Chord effect's Chance 0-100% do the same on the way in [D1]; the Groove Pool's Random adds timing jitter [D2].
Gap. velVar is global and only for hats, snare and perc; there is no chance at all.
Spec.
- types.ts: `Note = [midi, start, dur, vel, chance?]` with chance 0-1 (absent = 1). `isNote` already accepts 5 elements; `cleanNote` must carry element 4 through (today it rebuilds a strict 4-tuple and would drop it). Track gains `velDev?: number` (0-40 velocity steps) and `chance?: number` (track-wide default).
- ops.ts: `grid` gains `"chance": {"g": 0.6}` per char; `notes` and `sectionNotes` accept 5-tuples; `{"op":"chance","track":id,"value":0.7,"pattern"?: name}`; `{"op":"velDev","track":id,"steps":15}`.
- schedule.ts: per event and per repetition, `roll = rngTrack()`; skip when `roll >= (note[4] ?? track.chance ?? 1)`; `vel += round((rngTrack() * 2 - 1) * velDev)`; the per-track stream is `mulberry32(seed ^ hash(track.id))`. Determinism keeps the render identical between plays and lets the section cache stay valid (its key already includes the active tracks).
- contract.ts adds: "CHANCE: a g at chance 0.6 is a ghost that comes and goes. Put 0.5-0.8 on ghost hats, shaker offbeats, vocal-chop slices and arp tops; never on kick, clap or sub."
- Verify metric: transientsPerBeat.
Test. A hat grid of 16 x over an 8-bar section at chance 0.5 schedules between 40 and 88 of 128 events (binomial 3 sigma), deep-equal on two calls, while a kick with no chance keeps all 32; velDev 20 keeps every velocity within 20 of the written value and inside 1..127; a 5-tuple survives `cleanNote`.

## 4. Bounce, resample, reverse (commit any sound to a clip)
Origin. Live 12.2's Bounce to New Track and Bounce Track in Place render "post-FX and pre-mixer" to a new audio track named "(Bounce)" [D16][D37]; the Resampling input records the main output [D13]; Freeze renders a track to audio while volume, pan and sends stay live, Flatten commits it [D12]. Attack's six tricks: reverse reverb (bounce one bar, reverse it, move it one bar left, 100% wet 15 s reverb, bounce again, reverse the tail), delay and reverb tails as editable clips, a doubled first kick on every 8-bar loop, edits isolated from channel FX, beat juggling by an eighth-bar offset, reversed percussion grooves [D19]. EDMProd's loop is process, resample, reverse, repeat, and master-audio fills for transitions [D32]. Chris Lake prints everything to audio and reduces a project to six stems before the next round [D33]; his "most underrated" device is stock Overdrive for "vibrancy and lift, a bit more imperfect" [D26].
Gap. renderSong and renderWav render only the whole song; clips live in IndexedDB but nothing can render one track or one section into a clip, reverse a render, or freeze a track.
Spec.
- parse.ts BrainAction gains `type: "bounce"` with `tracks: ids | "drums" | "all"`, `section`, `bars?`, `tail?` (beats of reverb or delay tail to include), `reverse?`, `replace?`, `name?`, `place?: { section, bar }`.
- lib/engine/bounce.ts: `bouncePlan(song, tracks, section, bars, tail)` (pure, Node-testable) returns a mini Song: arrangement `[section]`, that section's bars clamped to `bars`, the chosen tracks `solo: true` and everything else unsoloed, loop null, and `master.bypass: true` (graph.ts skips comp, makeup, limiter and clipper when set, which is Ableton's post-FX pre-mixer). `renderTracks(song, plan)` runs renderOffline for `bars * 4 * beatSec + tail`; `reverseBuffer(buf)` flips every channel.
- useStudio.runAction: render, `putClip({ name: "<name> (Bounce)", bpm: song.bpm, durationSec })`, `cacheDecoded`, then applyOps: `addTrack { id: "bounce_<n>", role: "audio", duck: false, clip: { clipId, name, bpm: song.bpm, warp: true, lengthBeats: bars * 4, reverse } }`, `active` add in `place.section ?? section`; `replace: true` also removes the sources from that section's active list so undo restores them (freeze semantics).
- contract.ts adds: "BOUNCE: to commit a sound, reverse it, or make a transition from it, return action bounce; the studio renders those tracks through their FX for that section, stores the clip and adds an audio track you can warp, chop or reverse. Reverse reverb = bounce the vocal's first bar with tail 8 and reverse true, placed one bar before the drop."
Test (pure). `bouncePlan(seed, ["bass","sub"], "drop", 4, 2)` has arrangement `["drop"]`, drop bars 4, `audibleTracks` exactly bass and sub, `scheduleEvents(plan).totalBeats === 16`, and the derived addTrack op has role audio, clip.warp true, clip.lengthBeats 16, clip.bpm equal to song.bpm; `reverseBuffer` of a ramp is the reversed ramp.

## 5. Synced LFO modulation and per-track sidechain depth
Origin. Ableton's LFO device has nine waveforms (Sine, Up, Down, Triangle, Square, Random, Bin, Stray, Glider), Hz or beat-division rates, Phase 0-100%, Offset, Depth 0-100%, Jitter, Smooth, up to eight mapped targets; Shaper draws a breakpoint envelope; Envelope Follower has Gain, Rise, Fall [D7]. Auto Filter's LFO has Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square, Ramp Up, Ramp Down, Wander and S&H with Phase and Spin between channels and stepped quantization [D14]. Clip envelopes are modulation (relative, blue) that coexists with automation (absolute, red), and an unlinked envelope can loop at odd lengths such as 3.2.1 for polyrhythms [D11]. Attack ducks a ride cymbal with an LFO tool rather than the kick sidechain [D18]; Logic's Modulator at 1/4 with a triangle ramp gives sidechain-style pumping on pads [D27]. The process library prescribes a cutoff LFO synced to 1/8 or 1/16 for the Lake bass, pads and stabs at 2-4 dB of sidechain against 6 dB on the bass, and sub-only or none in garage (CORE, 30-bass).
Gap. Automation is ramps only; the trance gate is a fixed square; one sidechain depth applies to every duck:true track.
Spec.
- types.ts: `export type Mod = { target: "filter.cutoff" | "volume" | "pan" | "width" | "drive" | "delay.wet" | "reverb"; shape: "sine" | "tri" | "saw" | "ramp" | "square" | "sh" | "wander"; rate: "1n" | "2n" | "4n" | "8n" | "16n" | "8t" | "2m" | "4m"; depth: number; phase?: number; offset?: number; smooth?: number }`; `Track.mods?: Mod[]` (max 3); `Track.duck?: boolean | number` where a number is the depth in dB (0-14), true means master.sidechainDb, 0 or false means none.
- voices.ts: one `Tone.LFO` per mod, `sync()`ed to the transport with the rate as a division, min and max around the parameter's current value (cutoff depth in octaves, volume in dB, pan and width in units), started at transport 0 so phase is deterministic; sh and wander are a Tone.Part stepping seeded values at the rate. Modulation is relative: a section automating filter.cutoff 300 to 3000 keeps wobbling around the ramp.
- graph.ts: one duck Gain per distinct depth (`Map<number, Gain>`), each with the master release; tracks join the bus of their depth.
- ops.ts: `{"op":"mod","track":id,"mod":{...}}` (replaces by target) and `{"op":"clearMod","track":id,"target"?}`; `set duck 3` already works through setPath.
- contract.ts adds: "MOD: a synced LFO on a parameter. bass filter.cutoff sine 8n depth 1.5 is the Lake wobble; pad volume tri 1n depth 0.3 breathes; sh 16n on pan for stereo hats. duck can be a number: pads 3, stabs 4, bass 6, sub 0 in garage."
- Verify: pump for depth, centroidHz for a cutoff wobble on a 4-bar hear.
Test (pure). `modPlan(song)` resolves rates to beats (8n = 0.5, 8t = 0.333, 2m = 8) and rejects an unknown target with a skipped-op message; `duckGroups(song)` on a seed with `set duck 3` on the pad returns `{ 6: ["bass","sub",...], 3: ["pad"] }` and puts a duck:false track in no group.

## 6. Slice to MIDI and warp modes for uploads
Origin. Slice to New MIDI Track cuts at beat resolutions, transients or warp markers and produces "one note for each slice, arranged in a chromatic sequence" plus a Drum Rack chain per slice; Convert Drums, Melody and Harmony to MIDI read rhythm and pitch out of audio [D10]. Warp modes: Beats (Preserve transients or a grid, Transient Loop Off, Forward, Back-and-Forth, Transient Envelope 0-100) for drum loops, Tones (grain size) for vocals and basslines, Texture (grain plus Fluctuation) for pads and noise, Re-Pitch (turntable pitch follows tempo), Complex and Complex Pro (Formants, Envelope 128) for whole songs; the x2 and /2 buttons fix a wrong tempo estimate [D3]. Attack's Simpler fill uses Preserve 1/16 for a gate feel and 1/32 in the drop, Beats warping by default and Complex Pro for smooth playback [D20]. The process library wants the vocal teased filtered in the intro, exposed in the breakdown and chopped in the drop (40-harmony-hooks).
Gap. One GrainPlayer path (a Tones/Texture approximation) warps everything including drum loops; one-shots have a single `startSec`, so a chop means many clips or brain-side tricks; no transient slices.
Spec.
- types.ts AudioClip gains `warpMode?: "beats" | "tones" | "repitch"`, `slices?: number[]` (seconds, sorted) and `sliceMode?: "transient" | "beat" | "8" | "16"`. Default warpMode: beats when the ears report transientsPerBeat above 2 with low key confidence, tones otherwise.
- lib/ears/slices.ts (pure): `sliceAt(onsetEnv, fps, { by, bpm, n, sensitivity })` returns seconds; useUploads calls it on decode (hearBuffer already has the envelope) and stores `slices` for transient mode.
- ops.ts: `{"op":"slice","track":id,"by":"transient"|"beat"|"8"|"16","n"?: 8-32}` sets `clip.slices`, `clip.oneShot = true`, and writes pattern A as the chromatic sequence: note i is `[60 + i, quantizedStartBeat, sliceBeats, 100]`. The brain then reorders with notes or grid; midi 60 + i means slice i, exactly Ableton's mapping.
- voices.ts audio: with `slices` present, note midi 60 + i plays `player.start(t, slices[i], slices[i + 1] - slices[i])`; pitch comes from `clip.semitones` (a per-note pitch field is a later item).
- graph.ts: beats = one Player per slice segment scheduled on the song grid at rate 1 with a short fade (the Transient Envelope), tones = the current GrainPlayer, repitch = Player with playbackRate `song.bpm / clip.bpm` and no detune.
- contract.ts adds: "CHOPS: slice a vocal or loop (transient or 16), then write its pattern with midi 60 + slice index. warpMode beats for drum loops, tones for vocals, repitch for the pitched-up garage vocal."
Test. tests/ears.test.mjs: a synthetic click train with 8 evenly spaced onsets over 2 s gives 8 slice times within 10 ms of truth; tests/song.test.mjs: `slice by "8"` on a 4-beat clip yields 8 slices, 8 notes with midi 60..67 at 0, 0.5, 1.0 ... and `clip.oneShot === true`.

## 7. Note transforms: arp, chordify, snap, diatonic transpose, duration, strum, flam, euclid, ladder
Origin. Ableton MIDI effects: Arpeggiator (Up, Down, Converge, Diverge, Play Order, Chord Trigger, Random, Random Other, Random Once; Rate synced or ms; Gate above 100% for legato; Steps and Distance to 36 semitones; Repeats 1-2 "emulate the strumming of a guitar"), Chord (six shifts of 36 semitones, velocity 1-200%, Chance 0-100%, Strum up to 400 ms with Tension and Crescendo), Scale (Base, Transpose 36, Fold beyond six semitones), Pitch (in scale degrees), Note Length (Gate 50-200%), Velocity (Drive, Compand, Random, Fixed) [D1]. Live 12 MIDI Tools: Arpeggiate, Strum (Low, High, Tension), Ornament (flam and grace notes with Chance), Span (legato, tenuto, staccato), Velocity Shaper (envelope with Division), Euclidean (up to four voices with Rotation), Rhythm, Seed, Recombine, Time Warp [D6]. FL's piano roll has Riff Machine, Arpeggiate, Strum, Flam, Randomize, Chop [D34]; Logic's Note Repeater at 8d with a 63% velocity ramp makes ghost patterns and its Velocity Processor at 8:1 evens a performance [D27]. Attack: arps in up, down and up/down at 1/16, 1/8 or 1/8 triplets over one octave, with latch, for basslines that follow the chord root [D21][D23].
Gap. The brain retypes notes; grid covers one-pitch patterns; velocity op only scales; transpose is chromatic.
Spec (lib/song/transform.ts, pure, offline; the engine stays a player).
- `{"op":"arp","track":id,"from"?: chordsTrackId,"style":"up"|"down"|"updown"|"converge"|"diverge"|"order"|"random","rate":0.25|0.5|0.125|"8t","octaves":1-3,"gate":0.3-1.5,"repeats"?: n,"pattern"?: name,"seed"?: n}` reads the chord sounding at each step and emits one note per step.
- `{"op":"chordify","track":id,"shifts":[3,7,10]|"m7"|"m9"|"maj7"|"sus4"|"power","velocity"?: 0.8,"chance"?: 1,"strumMs"?: 0-400}` (names resolve through harmony.ts TEMPLATES).
- `{"op":"snap","track":id,"scale"?: "A minor"|"phrygian","fold"?: true}` snaps to the nearest scale tone, ties go down; only ever on request.
- `{"op":"transpose","track":id,"degrees":n}` is diatonic in song.key.
- `{"op":"duration","track":id,"scale"?: 0.5-2,"articulation"?: "legato"|"staccato"|"tenuto"}`.
- `{"op":"strum","track":id,"ms":5-60,"direction":"up"|"down","tension"?: -1..1}` and `{"op":"flam","track":id,"ms":10-30,"vel":0.4-0.7,"chance"?: 1}`.
- `{"op":"euclid","track":id,"steps":16,"hits":5,"rotate":0,"midi"?,"vel"?}` builds a grid string and reuses grid.
- `{"op":"ladder","track":id,"vel":[110,70,90,60]}` applies a per-16th-slot velocity map.
- contract.ts adds one line listing them: "TRANSFORMS rewrite a pattern in one op; prefer them over retyping notes."
Test. Am7 `[57,60,64,67]` held 4 beats: arp up at 0.25 gives 16 notes at i * 0.25 cycling 57, 60, 64, 67; converge gives 57, 67, 60, 64; random with seed 7 is deep-equal on two runs; chordify m7 on `[57]` gives `[57,60,64,67]` at one start; snap of `[58,61]` in A minor gives `[57,60]`; transpose degrees 2 of `[57,60,64]` gives `[60,64,67]`; euclid 16/5 gives `x..x..x..x..x...`; ladder repeats `[110,70,90,60]` across 16 hats.

## 8. Stutter and roll fills (MIDI-domain Beat Repeat)
Origin. Beat Repeat captures at an Interval from 1/32 to 4 bars with a Grid, Variation (Trigger, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, Auto), Chance 0-100%, Gate in 16ths, Pitch with Decay, and Mix, Insert or Gate output modes [D14]. Gross Beat maps 36 time and 36 volume envelopes over a 2-bar buffer for stutter, repeat, reverse, gate and scratch with Attack 0-500 ms, Release 0-1000 ms and Tension [D28]. Attack's pitch fill: Grid 1/8, Gate 8/16, Pitch Decay 100%, on/off automated [D20]. Logic's Scripter drum embellishments trigger rolls, drags and flams [D27]. The process library's accel roll is 8ths x4, 16ths x2, 32nds x2 with velocity 60 to 100%, and KETTAMA fills every 4 and 8 bars (10-arrangement).
Gap. fill roll is a fixed 8-hit clap crescendo; cut and riser are the only other devices; nothing retriggers what is already playing.
Spec.
- types.ts: `export type Fill = { kind: "stutter" | "roll" | "pitchRoll"; track: string | "drums" | "all"; atBar: number; beats: 1 | 2 | 4; grid: 0.5 | 0.25 | 0.125 | 0.0625 | "accel"; decay?: number; pitch?: number; chance?: number; insert?: boolean }`; `Section.fills?: Fill[]`; atBar negative counts from the end.
- schedule.ts: after tiling a section, for each fill capture the target tracks' events at the window start (or the last event before it), re-emit them on the grid inside the window with velocity `v * (1 - decay * i / n)`, a midi ramp of `pitch` across the window for melodic and audio tracks, seeded chance per repeat, and remove the originals when insert is true; the legacy `fill: "roll"` becomes sugar for `{ kind: "roll", track: "clap", atBar: -1, beats: 2, grid: 0.25 }`.
- ops.ts: `{"op":"fill","section":id,"fill":{...}}` and `{"op":"clearFills","section":id}`. With item 2's `every` plan, "a fill at the end of every 8 bars" is one op.
- contract.ts adds: "FILLS: stutter the drums for the last beat of the build at 32nds with decay 0.5; pitchRoll the vocal chop up 12 over 2 beats into the drop; accel = 8ths, 16ths, 32nds."
Test. An 8-bar section with a 4/4 kick and 16th hats plus `{ kind: "stutter", track: "drums", atBar: -1, beats: 1, grid: 0.0625, decay: 0.5, insert: true }` yields exactly 16 kick and 16 hat events at 0.0625 spacing in the last beat with descending velocities, no original hats inside that beat, and beats 1-3 of bar 8 untouched; accel over 2 beats yields 4 + 2 + 2 events at 8th, 16th and 32nd spacing.

## 9. Delay return, drum and low buses with glue
Origin. Return tracks process audio sent from many tracks, each with a Pre/Post send toggle, and sends on returns are disabled by default against runaway feedback; Group tracks are summing containers that host effects [D8]. SOS: returns for several ungrouped tracks with Pre sends for independent dry and wet, groups for a natural sub-mix such as drums [D25]. Drum Buss adds Drive, Boom, Transients and Crunch on the drum group [D14]. The process library: garage hat send to a 1/8 echo at 40% feedback at -25 dB, glue 2-4 dB on drums, mono below 120 Hz (20-drums, CORE); Lake's Overdrive for lift [D26].
Gap. One reverb return; delay is a per-track insert so tails cannot be shared or ducked; no drum bus, no low bus; the master compressor is the only glue.
Spec.
- types.ts: `Song.returns?: { reverb?: { size: number; damp: number; predelayMs?: number; hpfHz?: number; duck?: number }; delay?: { time: "8n" | "8n." | "16n" | "4n" | "8t"; feedback: number; hpfHz?: number; pingpong?: boolean; duck?: number } }`; `Fx.sends?: { reverb?: number; delay?: number }` (`fx.reverb` migrates to `fx.sends.reverb`; `fx.delay` stays an insert); `Song.buses?: { drums?: Bus; low?: Bus; music?: Bus }` with `Bus = { comp?: { threshold: number; ratio: number; makeup?: number }; drive?: number; eq?: { low; mid; high }; mono?: boolean; volume?: number }`; role map: drum roles to drums, bass and sub to low (mono true by default), everything else to music.
- graph.ts: three Tone.Gain buses with optional Compressor, Distortion and EQ3 feeding duck or master; returns are the existing Freeverb plus a FeedbackDelay or PingPongDelay with a highpass, each optionally routed through the duck bus; sends tap post-fader from the track's volume node (Ableton's Post default).
- AutoParam gains `"send.delay"` and `"send.reverb"` (alias of `reverb`); rollouts.ts scopeText for mix lists `returns.*` and `buses.*`.
- contract.ts adds: "RETURNS AND BUSES: reverb and delay are shared returns (fx.sends); drums, low and music are buses with glue. buses.drums.comp threshold -18 ratio 3 is the 2-4 dB drum glue; buses.low.mono keeps the sub centered; returns.delay 8n feedback 0.4 with hats sends.delay 0.06 is the garage echo."
Test. migrateSong moves `fx.reverb: 0.2` into `fx.sends.reverb`; `busOf(track)` maps kick to drums, sub to low, pad to music; `set buses.drums.comp.threshold -18` lands; `busPlan(song)` (pure) lists every audible track under exactly one bus and every send under a declared return.

## 10. Choke groups
Origin. A Drum Rack chain can join one of sixteen choke groups and "any chains that are in the same choke group will silence the others when triggered" [D9]; the 909 closed hat cutting the open hat is the pedal-hat language the process library describes (20-drums).
Gap. hat and openhat are independent voices; an open hat rings through the next closed hat.
Spec. `Track.choke?: string | null` (default "hats" for hat and openhat, none otherwise); voices.ts adds `Voice.choke(time)` that ramps `gate.gain` to 0 in 4 ms and restores it 10 ms later; graph.ts, when firing an event on a choke-group track, calls `choke` on the other voices in the group. Op: `set choke "hats"` or `null`.
Test. `chokePlan(schedule, song)` (pure): with open hats on the offbeat 8ths (decay 0.32 s, about 0.67 beats at 126) and closed hats on 16ths, every open hat still sounding at the next closed hat receives a choke event at that hat's beat; with `choke: null` on the hat track none are produced.

## 11. Sound presets per section (rack variations)
Origin. Racks hold up to 16 macros, store macro states as variations, randomize mapped macros, and switch chains through key, velocity or chain-select zones [D9]; the process library says open the filter in drops and close it in breakdowns (30-bass).
Gap. A section can choose a pattern per track but not a sound; a static per-section sound today is an automation ramp with from equal to to.
Spec. `Track.presets?: Record<string, { synth?: Partial<SynthParams>; fx?: Partial<Fx>; volume?: number }>`; `Section.sounds?: Record<string, string>`; graph.ts builds one voice per (track, preset used in the arrangement) and routes that section's events to it. Ops: `{"op":"preset","track":id,"name":"open","synth":{...},"fx":{...}}` and `{"op":"sound","section":id,"track":id,"preset":name|null}`.
Test. `voicesNeeded(song)` returns `bass:A` and `bass:open` when only drop2 uses "open"; `sectionCacheKey` changes when a section's sound changes and not otherwise.
