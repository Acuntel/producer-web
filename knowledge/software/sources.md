## Ableton Reference Manual, Live 12 (P)
- D1 Live MIDI Effect Reference: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/live-midi-effect-reference/ . Arpeggiator styles (Up, Down, Converge, Diverge, Play Order, Chord Trigger, Random, Random Other, Random Once), Rate sync or ms, Gate above 100% for legato, Steps and Distance to 36 semitones, Retrigger Off/Note/Beat, velocity Decay to Target, Hold, Pattern Offset, Repeats (1-2 "emulate the strumming of a guitar"); Chord six shifts of 36 semitones, velocity 1-200%, Chance 0-100%, Strum to 400 ms, Tension and Crescendo; Note Length Gate 50-200%; Pitch 128 semitones or 30 scale degrees, Block/Fold/Limit; Random Chance, Choices 1-24, Interval, Random/Alt, Add/Sub/Bi; Scale Base, Transpose 36, Fold beyond six semitones, 13x13 note matrix; Velocity Out Hi/Low, Clip/Gate/Fixed, Drive, Compand, Random; CC Control.
- D2 Using Grooves: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/using-grooves/ . Base, Quantize 0-100%, Timing, Random (per-voice jitter), Velocity -100 to +100 (negative inverts), Global Amount to 130%; grooves work on MIDI and on warped audio; Commit writes notes or warp markers; Extract Groove from the playing portion of any clip.
- D3 Audio Clips, Tempo, and Warping: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/audio-clips-tempo-and-warping/ . Beats (Preserve transients or grid; Transient Loop Off, Forward, Back-and-Forth; Transient Envelope), Tones (grain size, vocals and basslines), Texture (grain size, Fluctuation), Re-Pitch, Complex and Complex Pro (Formants, Envelope default 128); warp markers; x2 and /2 tempo fixes; auto-warp places a marker per bar.
- D4 Launching Clips (Follow Actions): https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/launching-clips/ . No Action, Stop, Play Again, Previous, Next, First, Last, Any, Other, Jump; Chance A and B; Follow Action Time in bars.beats.sixteenths; Linked mode fires at clip end or after the Multiplier loops; scene follow actions.
- D5 Editing MIDI: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/editing-midi/ . Chance 0-100% per note, probability randomization within an amount; Velocity Deviation as a signed range ("60 with +20 plays 60 to 80"); expression lanes; scale highlighting and Fold to Scale; MIDI Tools panels.
- D6 MIDI Tools: https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/midi-tools/ . Generators Rhythm, Seed, Shape, Stacks, Euclidean (up to four voices, Rotation); transformations Arpeggiate, Chop (to 64 parts), Connect, Glissando, LFO (MPE), Ornament (flam and grace with Chance), Quantize, Recombine, Span (legato, tenuto, staccato), Strum (Low, High, Tension), Time Warp, Velocity Shaper (envelope, Division).
- D7 Max for Live Devices: https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/max-for-live-devices/ . LFO nine waveforms (Sine, Up, Down, Triangle, Square, Random, Bin, Stray, Glider), Hz or beat divisions, Phase 0-100%, Offset, Depth, Jitter, Smooth, up to eight targets; Shaper breakpoint envelope; Envelope Follower Gain, Rise, Fall; Modulation vs Remote Control.
- D8 Mixing: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/mixing/ . Return tracks host effects for many tracks; sends on returns disabled by default against feedback; Pre/Post per return; Group tracks as summing containers with effects; crossfader with seven curves.
- D9 Instrument, Drum and Effect Racks: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/instrument-drum-and-effect-racks/ . Parallel chains; up to 16 macros (eight shown); macro variations; Rand; Key, Velocity and Chain Select zones on a 0-127 selector; Drum Rack choke, sixteen groups.
- D10 Converting Audio to MIDI: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/converting-audio-to-midi/ . Slice to New MIDI Track at beat resolutions, transients or warp markers, slicing presets, "one note for each slice, arranged in a chromatic sequence", a Drum Rack chain per slice; Convert Harmony, Melody and Drums to MIDI.
- D11 Clip Envelopes: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/clip-envelopes/ . Automation is absolute (red), modulation is relative (blue) and both coexist; unlinked envelopes loop at arbitrary lengths such as 3.2.1; transposition, volume and sample-offset envelopes; MIDI CC envelopes to 119.
- D12 Computer Audio Resources and Strategies (Track Freeze): https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/computer-audio-resources-and-strategies/ . Freeze renders a sample per clip plus the Arrangement; groups cannot be frozen; volume, pan and sends stay live; clips can still be trimmed, consolidated and dragged to audio tracks.
- D13 Recording New Clips (Resampling, Capture MIDI): https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/recording-new-clips/ . Resampling input records from other tracks or the main output into Samples/Recorded; Capture MIDI; MIDI Arrangement Overdub.
- D14 Live Audio Effect Reference: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/live-audio-effect-reference/ . Beat Repeat Interval 1/32 to 4 bars, Offset, Grid, Variation (Trigger, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, Auto), Chance 0-100%, Gate in 16ths, Pitch with Decay, Mix/Insert/Gate; Auto Filter LFO Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square, Ramp Up, Ramp Down, Wander, S&H, Phase and Spin, Steps quantization; Auto Pan-Tremolo; Drum Buss Drive, Boom, Transients above 100 Hz, Crunch.
- D15 Arrangement View: https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/arrangement-view/ . Consolidate, clip fades and crossfades, Duplicate Time, Delete Time, Insert Silence.

## Swing and groove practice (T, F)
- D29 IQSounds, Minimal Tech House Swing Settings in Ableton: https://iqsounds.com/blogs/news/minimal-tech-house-swing-settings-ableton . Groove amount 10-30%; 52-56% feel, 52-54% "tight and modern"; Swing 16 with moderate timing and low randomization; kick straight, main clap straight or nearly; swing closed hats, shakers, percussion, ghosts, possibly bass; velocity matters as much as timing.
- D30 FaderPro, How to Use Swing Rhythms in Music Production: https://blog.faderpro.com/techniques/how-to-use-swing-rhythms-in-music-production/ . Kick and hat straight as the fundamentals, offbeat elements swing; quantize at 25-50% to tighten without rigidity; Groove Pool templates then Commit.
- D31 Rapid Flow, MPC Groove Templates in Ableton (And Why They Are Not Enough): https://www.rapidflow.shop/blogs/news/mpc-groove-templates-ableton . Templates are timing maps shifting notes forward or backward off-grid (MPC60, MPC3000, SP-1200, 909); they move notes, not the clock underneath; no velocity data given.
- D32 EDMProd, Resampling Audio: 7 Fun Ways To Create Brand-New Sounds: https://www.edmprod.com/resampling-audio/ . Resampling input in Ableton, Edison on the master in FL, Bounce in Logic; reverse reverb and delay; bass resampling into a sampler with looping; process, resample, reverse, repeat; master-audio fills and transitions.
