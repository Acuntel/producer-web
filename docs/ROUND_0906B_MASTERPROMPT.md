# ROUND 2026-09-06 B: the $10,000 producer. Studio first, listen side second, hears you, can do what a human producer can do.

Brady's verdict on V10 (verbatim intent): the right side is too big, no chatbot, no keyboard, just a listen side; the left side must be thicker and is "a 5 dollar side project" with no design and wrong functionality; the listen side is not conversational and "needs to hear what I say"; the producer needs every capability a human producer has, including grabbing a one-second blip from any public source (YouTube, Reddit, anywhere), trimmed perfectly and accurately; it is not tested and does not work; do not stop until it is the product he asked for.

## Laws for this round
1. The studio is the product. Two thirds of the screen on desktop, designed like a real instrument: live meters, a real arrangement timeline with a playhead, interactive mixer, lanes that light up, transport. Nothing static, nothing that looks like a settings page.
2. The listen side is a voice surface, not a chat. No text box, no send button, no bubbles. It shows: the orb and its state, what it hears you say (large, live), what the producer is saying, a mic level meter that moves when you talk, a quiet transcript log, the verdict and A/B controls when they apply, and the two attach actions. Typed input exists only behind ?e2e=1 for the harness.
3. It must hear him. Every failure of the mic is visible and named: permission blocked, no device, speech service down, echo. A level meter proves the mic is live before any word is spoken. Barge-in stays. Errors are never swallowed.
4. Every capability a human producer has must be reachable by voice: analyze any record from a link or a file (links ON: ALLOW_LINK_DOWNLOAD=1 on Vercel and Railway per Brady's call), take a precise blip from any stem at a time he names ("the snare at 0:32", "one second of the vocal at 1:04"), tuned and trimmed on the transient, and drop it on the grid. The brain gets actions for analyze and slice, and the slicer trims to the transient with a pre-roll, ends on the decay or the next hit, snaps to zero crossings, fades, normalizes.
5. Screenshot at 1440 and 390 after every UI change and LOOK. A harness pass is not a product pass.

## Build order
A. Switch: ALLOW_LINK_DOWNLOAD=1 (Vercel + Railway). Worker: any public URL yt-dlp can resolve (YouTube, Reddit, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, direct audio files).
B. Listen side: components/ListenPanel.tsx replaces Conversation. hooks/useMicLevel.ts (getUserMedia + AnalyserNode). lib/voice/recognition.ts surfaces errors; useVoiceLoop exposes status + error. Layout: lg:grid-cols-[1fr_minmax(320px,24rem)].
C. Studio: components/Meters.tsx (master peak/RMS meter + spectrum canvas from Tone.Meter/Tone.FFT on the master), Timeline (proportional sections, energy shading, playhead, click to jump), Transport row (play/pause, position bar.beat, loop, undo/redo, bpm/key/lane chips), Mixer interactive (drag faders = volume ops, M/S buttons = local ops), TrackLanes with M/S and per-lane activity. Everything through the existing ops so the brain stays in sync (LOCAL MOVES).
D. Producer capabilities: op "slice" {fromClipId, startSec, endSec|lengthSec, role, id, name, tuneToKey?} resolved client side from the decoded stem buffer (transient-aligned trim, zero-crossing snap, fades, normalize) into a new one-shot clip; action "analyze" {url|name} starts a stems job by voice; the brain contract and the sampling skill describe both; the worker slicer gets the same trimming rules.
E. Gates: tsc, build, unit, e2e, accounts e2e, stems e2e (with a link), screenshots, deploy, prod harnesses, a real-Chrome check of mic permission and recognition on the prod origin, memory, report.
